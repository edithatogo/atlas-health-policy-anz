# Rural Generalist Medicine source-status note

Observed: 10 September 2026

## Current source picture

Rural Generalist Medicine was recognised as a field of specialty practice within General Practice in 2025. More recent college material reports that the ACRRM and RACGP Rural Generalist training programs have been accredited for specialist registration and that eligible practitioners may now apply through Ahpra.

The Medical Board FAQ available during this source review still used transitional wording referring to qualification accreditation as pending. That page may lag the later accreditation and registration announcements. It should not be used alone to encode a production eligibility rule.

## Policy Atlas treatment

The collection therefore separates:

- regulator recognition notices;
- regulator FAQs and implementation guidance;
- Ahpra registration-process guidance;
- college accreditation and registration notices;
- college training and advanced-skill requirements; and
- CHHHS credentialing and service-capability decisions.

A source may be current enough to inform design without being sufficient to determine a practitioner's registration, title, qualification equivalence or SoCP.

## Fail-closed operational rule

Before a CHHHS Rural Generalist model or automated rule is approved, the accountable owner must verify:

1. the current Medical Board list of recognised specialties and fields;
2. the current approved qualifications and transition arrangements;
3. the practitioner's current entry on the Ahpra register;
4. the relevant college qualification, training and advanced-skill evidence;
5. the CHHHS position scope and facility/service capability; and
6. the current delegated human decision.

Where regulator, Ahpra and college sources differ or appear asynchronous, the case must be routed for current-source verification. The system must not infer eligibility or use of a protected title from an older announcement, a college webpage, a historical credential or free text in the legacy system.

## Evidence ceiling

This note documents a source-currentness issue and the required verification response. It does not determine an individual's registration, qualification, title entitlement, competence or scope of clinical practice.
