## Initial classification

The ten STARS memoranda form a longitudinal comparator series rather than ten independent policies. They should be analysed as a sequence to identify how Metro North operationalised credentialing at STARS over time, including any changes in recognition, facility applicability, conditions, transitional arrangements and communication of approved SoCP.

The Lower GI Endoscopy memorandum is a separate thematic comparator and should be cross-referenced to procedure-specific SoCP, facility/service capability, monitoring and credentialing governance.
