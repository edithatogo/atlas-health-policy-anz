# Credentialing and model-SoCP implementation gates

The public Policy Atlas collection supports source discovery, comparison and change surveillance. It does not become a CHHHS rule base merely because a source has been indexed or a model has been published elsewhere.

## Gate 1: source identity and currentness

Confirm publisher, document identity, version, effective date, supersession status, accessibility and fixity. Where regulator, Ahpra and college sources differ or update asynchronously, route the matter for current-source verification rather than inferring a rule.

## Gate 2: authority and applicability

Classify each source as law, directive, mandatory standard, approved policy, guidance, professional standard, model scope, historical comparator or analyst interpretation. Record jurisdiction, affected professions, facilities and pathways.

## Gate 3: reuse rights

Record the licence, permission or applicable public-sector reuse basis. Public accessibility does not, by itself, establish permission to redistribute a binary or alter an external model.

## Gate 4: source-to-local crosswalk

Map each adopted clause, activity and evidence requirement to its source. Identify what is retained, modified, excluded or newly added for CHHHS and why.

## Gate 5: professional and service validation

Obtain relevant-peer and professional-stream review. Confirm position scope, facility and service capability, CSCF implications, support services, supervision, referral, retrieval and after-hours arrangements.

## Gate 6: equity and service context

Consider rural and remote access, workforce sustainability, First Nations governance and cultural safety, disability access, consumer implications and unintended administrative burden.

## Gate 7: local governance

Approve the local policy, procedure, schedule or model through the relevant CHHHS document and credentialing governance pathways. Confirm delegations, committee authority, conflicts, procedural fairness, review and appeal.

## Gate 8: transition and implementation

Define how existing scopes are mapped, what evidence may be reused, how exceptions are handled, how staff and practitioners are supported, and when the new model becomes effective. Avoid requiring complete reapplication where proportionate reconciliation is sufficient.

## Gate 9: digital and records controls

Confirm the authoritative system and record, access, retention, privacy, cyber security, integration, audit trail, business continuity, manual fallback and release-to-practise controls.

## Gate 10: surveillance and retirement

Assign a source steward and local model custodian. Monitor upstream changes, review incidents and exceptions, record model versions, manage transition, and retire superseded models without silently altering existing decisions.

## AI and automation boundary

Automation may find, extract, compare, map and draft. It cannot decide source authority, applicability, professional competence, facility capability, SoCP, conditions, approval, review, appeal or release to practise. Every operative local rule and individual decision requires accountable human approval.
