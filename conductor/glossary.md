# Glossary

- **Bronze**: immutable captured source evidence.
- **Silver**: reproducibly parsed and normalised source structure.
- **Gold**: atomic bitemporal policy assertions with provenance.
- **Platinum**: validated cross-jurisdiction comparisons and analytical products.
- **Diamond**: decision products, recommendations and candidate policy designs.
- **Conductor track**: bounded unit of requirements, plan, decisions, evidence and validation.
- **Compatibility mode**: recorded fallback that emulates a dependency interface without claiming native execution.
