## Implemented scope

The canonical repository is `edithatogo/atlas-health-policy-anz`. New Zealand is a first-class national jurisdiction (`NZ`). `ANZ` identifies a shared publisher's retrieval scope, not a new state or a determination that its documents are legally binding in either country. Original AU v1 inventories and completion receipts stay byte-for-byte unchanged. The added source inventory is a separately selected `anz-v1` collection; it does not reopen or redefine the frozen 28-surface AU census.

The typed catalogue contains 212 bodies/functions. It includes all members of nine independently enumerated official directory snapshots: 15 Australian National Boards, 18 NZ HPCA responsible authorities, 10 Australian external accreditation authorities, six accreditation committees, 16 specialist colleges, 15 NSW co-regulatory councils, eight AU health-complaints entities, eight AHSSQA service accreditors and four NZ designated auditing agencies. A directory match is only a registration coverage statement, never document-corpus completeness. The separate coverage contract records official evidence URLs and the observation date, 2026-09-05. Matching the count is insufficient: missing and substituted identities fail validation.

Other registered sources span national/state health policy and licensing, medicines/devices, pricing/funding, research ethics, aged care/disability, radiation, workplace safety, privacy, rights, legislation and professional guidance. These open-ended groups have an unknown denominator and an explicit remaining-scope ledger. We do not assert that all ANZ bodies or subordinate statutory functions have been discovered. Live domain-specific discovery and evidence review must resolve that ledger before such a claim is possible.

## Inference and framework rules

The graph projection contains registered bodies, source surfaces and publisher roles. It contains no inferred legal obligations, clinical recommendations, equivalences or compliance edges. Medsafe regulation and Pharmac funding are separate roles. Service accreditation and professional education accreditation are separate. AHSSQA directory membership does not imply authority for every scheme; NZ DAA designations also have different scopes and expiry dates. BSI Group NZ is the current listed name for the former HDANZ entity, not an additional fifth auditor. The Social Workers Registration Board is separately registered, not incorrectly added to the HPCA authority count.

NZ framework references include Ngā Paerewa NZS 8134:2021, HDC consumers' rights, HPCA, the Health Information Privacy Code with current amendments, Te Tiriti, NEAC ethics and HQSC clinical governance. They are retrieval references, not qualified executable conformance rubrics. Preserve Māori, whānau, Te Tiriti and Māori data sovereignty as distinct concepts. The spaCy phrase layer preserves macrons and exact offsets; it is not a qualified Māori-language statistical model and does not independently corroborate the same deterministic rules. Historical DHBs and abolished entities retain valid-time identity rather than becoming current independent jurisdictions.
