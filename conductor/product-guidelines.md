# Product Guidelines

1. Evidence before inference.
2. Comparability must be established before ranking or normative judgement.
3. Textual similarity is not policy equivalence.
4. Non-retrieval is not evidence of absence.
5. Instrument authority, scope, jurisdiction and valid time remain explicit.
6. Semantic methods generate candidates; provenance-bearing rules and validation establish claims.
7. Higher-tier analyses require qualified lower-tier prerequisites.
8. Confidence is evidence-derived, claim-level and explicitly reported; model self-confidence is not authority.
9. Strong evidence supports autonomous promotion; weaker/conflicted evidence is reported as needing verification rather than blocking unrelated work.
10. Pairwise equivalence is not presumed transitive.
11. Coverage percentages always name their denominator and observation cutoff.
12. Descriptive findings and normative recommendations remain separate layers.
13. Finite releases close when their declared acceptance contracts pass; optional refinement moves to the next release.
14. Generated policy recommendations remain traceable to evidence and explicit normative assumptions.
