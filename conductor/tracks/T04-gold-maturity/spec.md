# T04 — Gold assertions and concept maturity


## Objective
Create the canonical Gold policy model and verified atomic assertion corpus.

## Must requirements
- Versioned schema for actor, modality, action, object, condition/trigger, timeframe, exception, setting, authority, bindingness, valid time, observation time, source span and evidence state.
- Controlled concept identifiers/synonyms with provenance; terminology match never establishes policy equivalence.
- Deterministic/rule-based extraction where reliable; generative extraction only with exact source-span grounding and schema validation.
- Independent assertion verification path.
- Explicit states including confirmed, uncertain, conflicting, not-found, source-unavailable, superseded and not-applicable as appropriate.
- Frozen adjudicated benchmark with difficult modality/scope/time/authority cases.
- Reproducible Gold rebuild from Silver/Bronze.

## Gate G
No Platinum production until schema/benchmark/source-span/authority/temporal/uncertainty requirements pass.
