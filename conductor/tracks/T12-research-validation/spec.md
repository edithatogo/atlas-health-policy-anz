# T12 — Research validation and outcome-linked analysis


## Objective
Validate the system externally and develop higher-order research only after comparative validity is established.

## Planned analyses
- corpus/structural variation;
- policy consensus and unwarranted variation;
- temporal change and diffusion/genealogy;
- administrative burden and complexity;
- sociotechnical/governance orientation;
- rural/remote feasibility and equity;
- scope-of-practice/legal-policy gaps;
- outcome linkage and quasi-experimental analyses only where identification is defensible.

Causal claims are explicitly out of scope unless a study-specific design and assumptions pass a separate research gate.
