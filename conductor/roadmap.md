# Roadmap

The roadmap is maturity-gated, not merely chronological. No downstream medallion implementation starts until the preceding scoped release is qualified. Planning, schemas, fixtures and benchmark design may occur early; production derivation may not.

## Phase 0 — Foundation
- **T00** Conductor/context, Git traceability, ecosystem/dependency governance. Active.

## Phase 1 — Bronze to maturity
- **T01** Australian source census, source taxonomy, rights registry and Hugging Face publication contracts.
- **T02** Bronze acquisition/preservation/publication implementation and Bronze v1 maturity qualification.

**Gate B:** declared source census closed for v1 scope; every item has disposition; all captured originals fixity/provenance/rights states; recoverability proven; rights-qualified public HF Bronze dataset remotely verified. Silver production remains blocked until Gate B passes.

## Phase 2 — Silver to maturity
- **T03** Silver parsing, structure, citations and lineage; parser benchmarks and differential validation.

**Gate S:** all in-scope Bronze formats have qualified parsers; extraction loss accounted; lineage reversible; Silver rebuild reproducible; benchmark thresholds pass.

## Phase 3 — Gold to maturity
- **T04** Canonical bitemporal policy assertion/concept model, extraction and verification.

**Gate G:** assertion schema stable/versioned; representative benchmark passes; source-span/authority/time lineage complete; uncertainty/abstention states validated; Gold rebuild reproducible.

## Phase 4 — Platinum to maturity
- **T05** Cross-jurisdiction equivalence, contradiction, comparability and Platinum qualification.
- **T06** Comparison assurance, model/method triangulation and benchmark laboratory.
- **T08** Versioned analytical framework registry and plug-in projections.

**Gate P:** comparisons pass scope/authority/time/coverage gates; benchmarked methods/models have manifests; disagreement retained; framework versions pinned; replay produces materially identical outputs.

## Cross-cutting capability development
- **T07** Agents, skills and workflows: typed contracts, fixtures, failure states, orchestration and evaluation for T01–T06/T08.

## Reproducible gap-analysis platform
- **T09** Programmatic gap-analysis engine and institutional adapter contract. This track explicitly targets:
  1. independent repetition/verification/validation of any published gap analysis;
  2. comparisons against one or more policies across one or more jurisdictions;
  3. re-computation when policies, procedures, guidelines, standards or analytical frameworks change;
  4. programmatic institutional PPG gap analysis against state/national/selected comparator PPGs.

## Living system and interfaces
- **T10** Atlas/API/CLI/MCP/Space interfaces over qualified datasets.
- **T11** Continuous source/framework/model drift detection, lineage-aware invalidation and versioned re-analysis.
- **T12** External research validation, policy diffusion/temporal studies and outcome-linked/causal analyses where defensible.

## Deferred application products
Only after Platinum maturity: recommendation synthesis, candidate policy wording, implementation options, policy-frontier narratives and institution-specific decision support. These remain derived products with human consequential-interpretation gates.
